Common Questions

New to 3D printing? Here are answers to some of the most commonly asked questions.

What is 3D printing?

3D printing is a process that creates physical objects layer by layer using a digital design.

What material should beginners use?

PLA filament is usually best for beginners because it is easy to print and affordable.

How expensive are 3D printers?

Beginner printers can start around £150-£300, while advanced professional printers cost much more.

How long does a print take?

Small models may take under an hour, while larger prints can take several hours or longer.

Can I design my own models?

Yes. Software such as Tinkercad, Fusion 360, and Blender can be used to create custom models.
